Who we are

Every wedding is a story of its own

Chayamughi is a team of photographers, cinematographers and editors who believe a photograph should hold the feeling of a moment, not just its likeness.

We believe every wedding is a story of its own, shaped by the people, emotions and little moments that make it unique. Through meaningful relationships and a thoughtful approach, we work closely with each family to create photographs and films that feel personal, timeless and true to their day.

Across the length and breadth of South India, we’ve travelled far and wide to capture the beauty of every region, culture and ritual.

Our story

From one belief to a family of storytellers

Founded by Sajith Moothakurambu, a photographer, writer and artist, Chayamughi grew from one simple belief into a team that families across South India trust with their most important days.

01

The belief

Photography is more than taking pictures. It’s about capturing the essence of a moment and preserving it for years to come, so you can revisit it with a smile on your face.

02

The journey

Over two decades behind the lens, travelling all over South India to photograph weddings, family ceremonies, portraits and children, and learning the unique beauty of each region along the way.

03

Today

A team of about ten: in-house photographers, cinematographers, hybrid photographers and editors, with our own music composer. Everything stays in-house, so every project is delivered on time.

A wedding day, through our eyes

Where we are when it matters

Families often ask what we actually do on the day. Here’s how a wedding unfolds from behind our cameras, from first light to the final cut.

  1. Dawn

    Before the first guest

    We arrive while the house is still waking up, learning the light, the space and the faces that matter most.

  2. Morning

    Getting ready

    Jasmine being pinned, a mother adjusting a pleat, a nervous laugh in the corner. We stay close, and quiet.

  3. The muhurtham

    The moment everyone waits for

    When the thali is tied, the whole hall holds its breath. Our cameras are already exactly where they need to be.

  4. Afternoon

    Between the rituals

    The sadya, the cousins, the grandparents’ stories. The best moments often happen when nobody is posing.

  5. Evening

    The last light

    Goodbyes, tired smiles, hands still holding. We stay until the day is truly done.

  6. Weeks later

    The story takes shape

    Our editors shape the photographs and films and our composer writes your theme music, until the day plays back the way it felt.
