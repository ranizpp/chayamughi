About Chayamughi

We’re not just photographers. We’re storytellers.

Chayamughi is a team of photographers, cinematographers and editors who believe a photograph should hold the feeling of the moment, not just its likeness. We work with each family to shape coverage around their own day.

From Kochi to Bengaluru, we’ve travelled across South India to capture the beauty of every region and every ritual.
