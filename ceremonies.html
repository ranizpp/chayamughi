Every family’s story is written in ceremonies. Here are the six we capture, in the order life brings them, from a baby’s first taste of rice to the wedding day.

First taste

It begins with a single grain of rice.

Chapter 01

Choroonu

Your baby’s first taste of rice, with the whole family gathered around.

An intimate but significant milestone, and often the very first ceremony in a family album. We capture the first feeding, the blessings and every little reaction.

We work quietly and patiently, so the ritual stays calm for the baby and natural for everyone else.

What we capture

  • First rice-feeding ritual
  • Candid baby moments
  • Family and elders’ blessings
  • Family portraits

Milestone moments

Then come the birthdays, counted by the stars.

Chapter 02

Pirannal

A birthday by the star, with the sadya and the little one at the centre.

Children don’t pose on cue, so we take our time. We stay through the rituals, the sadya and the play.

We wait for the candid moments that make the day, and make sure the whole family finds its way into the story too.

What we capture

  • Rituals and blessings
  • Candid children’s photography
  • Sadya and family gathering
  • Family portraits

Becoming

One morning, a sacred thread and a new beginning.

Chapter 03

Upanayanam

The sacred thread ceremony, documented ritual by ritual.

A ceremony rich in meaning and detail, and the moment a child steps into a new role. Our team understands the sequence of rituals, so nothing important is missed.

The homam, the priest’s guidance, the sacred thread and every blessing from the elders are recorded in full.

What we capture

  • Complete ritual documentation
  • Homam and sacred thread rites
  • Elders and family portraits
  • Ceremony film

Chapter 04

Engagement

The day two families formally come together, and your first day as a promised couple.

From the ring exchange to the elders’ blessings, we document the ceremony as it unfolds, without interrupting the rituals.

We also set aside time for your first portraits together, relaxed and unposed, so they feel like the two of you.

What we capture

  • Ring exchange and ceremony
  • Family and elders’ blessings
  • Candid moments of the gathering
  • Couple portraits
